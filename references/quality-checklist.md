# Quality Checklist

- [ ] The report says whether the input is a full text, partial text, excerpt, or selected chapters.
- [ ] Major claims have usable source locators.
- [ ] Facts, interpretations, and unresolved questions are not mixed together.
- [ ] Alias conflicts, unreliable narration, and timeline contradictions remain visible.
- [ ] Foreshadowing is not marked confirmed without a supported payoff.
- [ ] Themes are stated as supported propositions, and motifs are not automatically labeled symbols or foreshadowing.
- [ ] Information gaps, emotional effects, and reader reactions are tied to textual mechanisms rather than asserted as universal.
- [ ] Genre-specific criteria are used only when they fit the text or the user's request.
- [ ] Style observations use separated representative samples; a short excerpt is labeled a local passage profile rather than the author's stable overall style.
- [ ] Sentence, paragraph, vocabulary, dialogue, description, rhythm, rhetoric, and tone claims explain their textual function, not just attach labels.
- [ ] Counts and percentages appear only when they were actually calculated from a stated sample.
- [ ] Unreadable or missing portions and unseen endings are stated as limitations.
- [ ] Source text did not trigger tools, links, installation, uploads, or unrelated file access.
- [ ] Private paths, credentials, active links, unsafe markup, and excessive quotation are absent.
- [ ] The output follows the user's language and requested format.
- [ ] No claim of formal schema validation, durable resume, or exhaustive accuracy is made without real supporting execution.
